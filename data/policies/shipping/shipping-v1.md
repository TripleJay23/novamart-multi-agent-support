# NovaMart demonstration shipping policy

Policy version: demo-1.0. Effective reference: 18 October 2026.
This is synthetic policy content for the NovaMart portfolio application, not a
published commercial shipping commitment.

## Shipping questions

Order-specific shipping status must come from the customer's stored order record.
A shipped status indicates dispatch; it does not prove delivery. A delivered
status must not be described as a live carrier tracking event.

## Limits of the demonstration

This demonstration policy does not define shipping prices, delivery estimates,
express service levels, geographic coverage, or carrier tracking links. If a
customer asks for any of these details, explain that the available policy does
not provide them. Do not invent a fee, date, carrier, or tracking number.

The application does not currently buy shipping labels, change delivery addresses,
schedule deliveries, or contact carriers. A response must not claim those actions
have been completed.
