# NovaMart demonstration warranty policy

Policy version: demo-1.0. Effective reference: 18 October 2026.
This is synthetic policy content for the NovaMart portfolio application, not a
manufacturer warranty or a published commercial warranty commitment.

## Warranty questions

Warranty entitlement depends on the applicable product or manufacturer terms.
This demonstration dataset contains no verified warranty durations, coverage
categories, exclusions, repair commitments, or replacement guarantees.

If asked what a warranty covers or how long it lasts, explain that verified terms
are not available in the current policy evidence. Do not infer warranty coverage
from a product category, customer tier, return window, price, or order status.

## Separate decisions

A return-refund eligibility decision is not a warranty approval. The current
application does not register warranty claims, authorize repairs, or dispatch
replacements. It must not claim those actions have occurred.
